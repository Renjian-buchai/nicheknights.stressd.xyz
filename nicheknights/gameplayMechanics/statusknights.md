# Status Knights

Status very status.

| Status | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Cold/Freeze | ASPD reduced by 30 when cold. Freezes when hit by a second instance of cold. Disables the unit, and RES is reduced by 15 when frozen. |
| Fear | The enemy will flee from the unit which Feared them instead of following their route and becomes unable to be blocked. |
| Lure | The enemy will move towards the position they are Lured into instead of following their route and becomes unable to be blocked. |
| Paralysis | Always inflicted in up to three instances; each instance interrupts the victim's normal attack and is permanent unless if the victim has Status Resistance, where they periodically lose one Paralysis instance. |
| Stun | Disables the unit; Stunned friendly units are unable to block enemies. |
